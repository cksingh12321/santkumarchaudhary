Recognition

Honours & Awards

Public recognition of Dr. Sant Kumar Chaudhary’s contribution to education and rural development in India.

Bharat Gaurav Award · British Parliament, 2019

In July 2019, Dr. Sant Kumar Chaudhary was conferred the Bharat Gaurav Award by the NRI Welfare Society of India at the House of Commons, British Parliament, London, in recognition of his contribution to education and rural development in India.

Honorary Doctorate · European Union university consortium

He is reported to be the 7th recipient of an honorary doctorate conferred by a European Union university consortium that has, in its first hundred years, recognised only ten individuals worldwide for their contribution to education and social work.

BRICS recognition

The Shankara Group, under his leadership, secured first place from India at an international educational-institutions seminar organised under the BRICS framework.

Chancellor’s Nominee — Government of Rajasthan

The Government of Rajasthan has named Dr. Chaudhary a Chancellor’s Nominee in the Higher Education department, an official advisory appointment listed in the Lok Bhavan public record.

Leadership positions

The principal positions Dr. Chaudhary currently holds:

  • Chairman cum Managing Trustee — S.K. Chaudhary Educational Trust
  • Chairman, Board of Trustees — Shankara Group of Institutions
  • Chairman, Board of Governors — Shankara Group of Institutions
  • Chairman — Shankara Institute of Technology, Jaipur

Distinguished company

The Board of Governors he chairs at the Shankara Group includes leaders he has drawn together from industry, government, and academia in India and abroad — among them the former CMD of Power Grid Corporation of India, Deans of US universities, the President (Corporate) of Reliance Industries, and members of the National Highways Authority of India. The standing of the board is itself a measure of the regard in which his work is held.
