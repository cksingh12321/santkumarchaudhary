Vision & Philosophy

“To extend helping hands towards those regions that most require it.”

The guiding principle that shaped every institution Dr. Sant Kumar Chaudhary has built.

His philosophy of education

For Dr. Sant Kumar Chaudhary, education has never been an end in itself. It is the means by which a young person from a rural village becomes capable — materially, intellectually, and morally — of changing the conditions around them. Two lines, drawn from his own work, capture this view:

“Right education should make us know that God is the truth.”
“The first fruit of education must be humanity and self-control.”

On leading an institution

“I believe in giving utmost importance to each and every part of the institution — from students, to academicians, to the administration — and it gives you the correct visibility.” — Dr. Sant Kumar Chaudhary, Chairman, Shankara Group of Institutions

His chosen geography

Dr. Chaudhary chose, deliberately, to build in places that other educational entrepreneurs avoid — the rural and backward pockets of Bihar, Rajasthan, Delhi, Maharashtra and Tamil Nadu. The choice is part of the philosophy. A premier institution in a metropolitan centre would have an easier life; one in a backward district changes more lives.

His method

He has paired education with the things that make education possible — health, livelihood, and dignity. The Trust under his leadership operates schools and colleges, yes, but also an eye hospital, a Krishi Vigyan Kendra, watershed-development programmes, and a small group of magazines on agriculture, technology, education and health. Each strand reinforces the others.
