Biography

The life and journey of Dr. Sant Kumar Chaudhary

Founder of the S.K. Chaudhary Educational Trust · Chairman cum Managing Trustee of the Shankara Group of Institutions, Jaipur.

Early life

Dr. Sant Kumar Chaudhary was born in Basaith Chainpura village, Benipatti block, Madhubani district, in the Mithila region of Bihar. He grew up in a family with a quiet, settled commitment to public life: his grandfather took part in India’s freedom movement and founded the school in their village, and his father, Shri Dukh Mohan Chaudhary, served as a school teacher.

Education

His schooling began at the government school in his native village. He went on to study at C. M. Science College, Darbhanga, completing his Intermediate and graduation by 1979, and in 1980 moved to Delhi to pursue a B. Pharma degree.

Early career — from government service to founding the Trust

Dr. Chaudhary’s working life began in government. He served first with the Government of Maharashtra Secretariat, and was later appointed Officer on Special Duty (OSD) to the Governor of Rajasthan. He eventually resigned from government service to devote himself, full-time, to the institution-building work that would become the Shankara Group.

During this transition he drew spiritual guidance from the Kanchi Kamakoti Shankaracharya — the connection that gave the family of institutions their name and their religious-cultural frame of reference.

Founding the S.K. Chaudhary Educational Trust (2001)

In 2001, Dr. Sant Kumar Chaudhary founded the S.K. Chaudhary Educational Trust, a service body created with one stated aim — “to extend helping hands towards those regions that most require it.” From the outset the Trust was conceived not as a single college but as a multi-pronged effort spanning education, healthcare, agriculture and rural development.

The Trust’s very first institution was an intermediate-level school in his native village of Basaith Chainpura. Its first hospital was an eye hospital in Madhubani, named after the Shankaracharya — the institution that today operates as the Kanchi Shankara Eye Hospital.

Building the Shankara Group

Under his leadership the Trust established the Shankara Group of Institutions at Kukas, near Jaipur — a cluster of professional colleges covering engineering, management, teacher-education, and nursing. Alongside the Jaipur campus, the Trust runs primary, high and intermediate schools and degree colleges in its home region of Bihar, and works in Maharashtra, Tamil Nadu and Delhi.

Beyond the classroom

His work has never been confined to the campus. The Trust he leads also runs the Kanchi Shankara Eye Hospital, the Krishi Vigyan Kendra at Chanpura, and a portfolio of watershed-development, soil-testing and seed-multiplication programmes serving farming communities.

Today

Dr. Sant Kumar Chaudhary continues to serve as Chairman cum Managing Trustee of the Trust and chairs both the Board of Trustees and the Board of Governors of the Shankara Group. The Trust now runs around 51 institutions across India — of which roughly 35 are educational — and has partnerships extending into Europe. He was conferred the Bharat Gaurav Award at the British Parliament in 2019, and the Government of Rajasthan has named him a Chancellor’s Nominee in its higher-education list.

His own stated goal is to take the Trust’s footprint to 101 institutions worldwide, to establish a national university in the Mithila region, and to add Medical, Ayurvedic and Homeopathic colleges in Madhubani.
